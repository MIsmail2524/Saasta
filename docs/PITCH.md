# Saasta for stores: pitch kit

## One-liner

> Shoppers now find clothes by photo and reel, not keywords. Saasta makes sure they find **your** store, and tells
> you what they want next.

## The 30-second pitch

"Your customers see an outfit on Instagram, screenshot it and ask 'where is this from?'. Search engines send them to
whoever they index, often a reseller or a copy. Saasta is a visual shopping search built for Pakistan: when
someone searches with a photo that matches your product, you show up first as a verified partner with a trust badge.
You can also add 'search by photo' to your own site with one line of code, and every week we show you what shoppers
in your category searched for and couldn't find. Listing is free. Want me to set it up for you today?"

## Who to pitch first

1. Instagram-first fashion brands with a Shopify site (eastern wear, khussa, jewellery, bags): fastest to onboard.
2. Mid-size local brands with large catalogs, where text search on their site is weak.
3. Marketplace sellers who also run their own site.

## Demo script (5 minutes)

1. Open saasta.pk and paste a screenshot of one of *their* products from Instagram.
2. Show where it currently appears: resellers, copies, price spread. "These sellers are taking your demand."
3. Show the trust score on a suspicious seller: "Your real customers get warned about fakes."
4. Open the store dashboard and import their Shopify catalog live (2 minutes).
5. Search again: their product now shows as a **Partner** exact match.
6. Show the widget snippet and the Demand radar.

## Objection handling

- **"We already have Google Shopping."** Great, Saasta also reads Google. But Google doesn't show trust signals,
  doesn't cover Instagram sellers, and doesn't give you demand data. We're additive and free to start.
- **"Will this send traffic to competitors?"** Your products appear for searches that match *your* items. The widget
  on your site only shows *your* catalog.
- **"Do you send our data to other AI companies?"** No. Saasta runs its own models on its own servers.
  Your photos are only used to train our models if you opt in.
- **"Is our data safe?"** We only read your public catalog. Widget keys are locked to your domain. Insights are
  aggregated and anonymous.
- **"We don't have a developer."** Shopify import is one click. The widget is one line in your theme.

## Metrics to report back to stores (monthly)

Clicks to your store, widget searches, top products clicked, unmet-demand queries in your category, typical price
band shoppers see for your category.
